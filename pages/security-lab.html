    Quick Inspection Checklist & Session Summary

    Keep these four verification cues in mind across every scenario in the simulator:

    • Check the Sender Domain: Verify the exact domain after the @ symbol rather than trusting the display name alone.
    • Inspect the Link Destination: Look at the true root domain right before the first single / in the URL preview.
    • Question Artificial Urgency: Be skeptical of countdowns, account suspension threats, or unexpected prize/payment claims.
    • Never Share Credentials or MFA Codes: Legitimate support teams never ask you to submit passwords or one-time codes through a link.
